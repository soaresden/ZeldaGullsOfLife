The Legend of Zelda: Gulls of Life - v0.1 (demo)
===============================================
"The Ballad of the Seagull"
A Game Boy Color hack of The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages, by soaresden
https://soaresden.github.io/ZeldaGullsOfLife/
https://github.com/soaresden/ZeldaGullsOfLife


The story
---------
The Wind Fish woke up and Koholint Island vanished... but not entirely.
Marin made a wish: to have wings and fly beyond the sea. She comes back as a
seagull, and finds Link washed up on an archipelago where nobody dreams
anymore.

Gulls of Life: "gulls" sounds like "goals". You make your dreams come true
instead of staying in them, and you keep going no matter what.


What this is
------------
A full hack and an unofficial sequel to Link's Awakening, not a level edit:

- a new story, with Marin as your companion: she talks, teases, and always
  has something to say (even to the bosses);
- new islands, towns, characters and dialogue;
- original music, composed note by note for the game;
- a hand-made opening film and title screen;
- new mechanics: dreaming and waking don't play by the same rules;
- real temples, each built around one key item, with bosses that talk back;
- minigames inspired by other video games.

English and French are both in the same ROM: you choose when you wake up on
the beach, and your save remembers it.


This version: 0.1 (demo, work in progress)
------------------------------------------
v0.1 contains the opening film, the whole first chapter (Mist Island and the
town of Port-Veille), the first temple (the Dark Lighthouse) and its boss,
the Port-Veille shop and a first minigame. It ends on a cliffhanger.

The rest of the game is in development.

Note: the title screen shows "v1.0/1" (the project's internal numbering). It
is the same demo as release v1.0/1.1 on GitHub.


How to play
-----------
1. You need your own ROM of The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages (USA):
     Legend of Zelda, The - Oracle of Ages (USA).gbc
     CRC32  3800a387
     MD5    c4639cc61c049e5a085526bb6cac03bb
     SHA-1  880374fb978b18af4aa529e2e32f7ffb4d7dd2f4
   No ROM is provided.
2. Apply gulls_of_life.bps with a BPS patcher, for example Rom Patcher JS
   (https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/), Flips or beat.
3. Play the patched ROM (CRC32 b9011dfa) in a Game Boy Color emulator.


Controls
--------
  Select            the map
  Hold Select       Marin has something to say
  A by a bed        sleep
  The rest is the usual Oracle of Ages controls.
